Findings

A finding is a result worth writing up, good or bad. Of 30 so far, 2 are confirmed, 7 are still being checked and 21 were ruled out.

Confirmed

F01A stock's recent path predicts how much it will move next week

How long it has been since a stock's 60-day high and low, how far it sits below the high, and how much of its recent move came on a handful of big days together forecast how much it will move over the next one to two weeks. Its own past volatility doesn't capture this. It predicts the size of the move, not the direction.

It held on 2021 data it was never tuned on, and it isn't explained by earnings dates or by simply being down from the high. It also shows up in Europe, more weakly.

Possible use: sizing positions or pricing options. Nothing has been built to trade it yet.

F03Recent extreme days point the same way, but it's the same signal

The number and timing of a stock's recent extreme days also forecast next week's volatility, and that held on 2021 data. Once F01 is accounted for, though, it adds nothing new, so it's treated as part of F01.
