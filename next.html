What's next

The research side works. The next steps are about turning a confirmed signal into something usable, and giving the system more to work with.

Not done yet

  • No finding is traded, and there's no code to trade one.
  • Whether the confirmed finding still makes money after trading costs hasn't been checked yet.
  • Only one computer does the work. The team's other computers aren't connected yet.
  • Requests typed in by people don't jump the queue yet.

Being built

  1. People first. A request from someone on the team ("test this tonight") goes to the front of the queue. If no test fits it, the system writes a short report saying why.
  2. Backtesting. A step that trades confirmed findings on past data, costs included.
  3. Re-checking every day. Past findings get re-run on new data each day, so one that stops working is caught.
  4. More machines. The team's other computers join, each reporting how busy it is.
